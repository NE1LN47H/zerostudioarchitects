The studio

Zero Studio is an architecture practice working across residential, cultural and commercial briefs. We keep the team small, so the architects you meet are the architects who draw your building.

Each project starts on site, with the light, the ground and the people who will use the space.

Our approach

Three principles guide every drawing we make.

Start with the site

We study light, climate and context before we draw a line.

Use fewer materials

A short list of honest materials, detailed well, ages better than a long one.

Build it with you

You see drawings, models and costs at every stage, so every decision is clear.
